D12 STRANGE OMENS
1Every candle in the village gutters out at
once, though there is no wind.
2A white stag crosses the road and stops to
stare at the party before vanishing.
3The church bell rings by itself at midnight.
4Birds fall silent for an hour; when they sing
again, it is a song nobody recognises, and
the oldest villager weeps to hear it.
5Milk sours in every pail overnight.
6A stranger arrives asking for a person who
died here long ago, and describes them
perfectly.
7Frost forms in the shape of a hand on the
inn's door.
8The river runs backwards until dawn.
9Every dog in town turns to face the north
and will not be called away.
10A child draws a map of a place she has never
seen, and it is accurate.
11Moths gather on the party's packs and
cannot be shaken off.
12The moon rises red, and the well water tastes
of iron.
