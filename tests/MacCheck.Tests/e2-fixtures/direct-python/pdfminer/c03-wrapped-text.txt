Every candle in the village gutters out at
once, though there is no wind.

A white stag crosses the road and stops to
stare at the party before vanishing.

The church bell rings by itself at midnight.

Birds fall silent for an hour; when they sing
again, it is a song nobody recognises, and
the oldest villager weeps to hear it.

5 Milk sours in every pail overnight.

A stranger arrives asking for a person who
died here long ago, and describes them
perfectly.

Frost forms in the shape of a hand on the
inn's door.

The river runs backwards until dawn.

Every dog in town turns to face the north
and will not be called away.

10

A child draws a map of a place she has never
seen, and it is accurate.

11 Moths gather on the party's packs and

cannot be shaken off.

12

The moon rises red, and the well water tastes
of iron.
