# c08-heading-two-result-columns

One roll, two outputs: a heading whose two words name the two result columns, a free-text column and a right-aligned
signed-modifier column (+30 ... −30 with a real minus sign). TableForge's "parallel outputs" interpretation should turn
this into two result sets, Difficulty and Modifier, sharing the same ranges.
Exercises: multiple result columns, column gaps vs single spaces, signed numbers with U+2212.
